Bhai, ye rahe AI newsletter ya technical discussions ke liye super-fresh, cutting-edge AI **buzzwords** unki 1-line crisp definitions ke saath:
## 1. Test-Time Compute (Inference Scaling)
Optimization paradigm where additional compute is spent during the inference phase, allowing models to "think longer" and solve complex reasoning problems without increasing model size.
## 2. Representation Engineering (RepE)
Top-down control technique that monitors and manipulates high-level internal cognitive states of AI models to regulate safety, truthfulness, and tone without retraining.
## 3. World Models
AI architectures that build internal, physics-aware 3D simulations of the physical world, enabling autonomous agents to predict spatial outcomes and plan complex actions.
## 4. Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs)
Advanced interpretability tool used to untangle millions of overlapping concepts inside neural networks into individual, human-understandable features.
## 5. Model Merging (Weight Averaging)
Technique for combining multiple specialized LLMs into a single powerful model by mathematically blending their parameters without additional training overhead.
## 6. Embodied AI
Interdisciplinary field fusing AI models with physical hardware (like robotics or spatial computing) to interact dynamically with real-world environments.
Ye saare words solid technical context rakhte hain aur newsletter ke readers ko ekdum insider vibe denge!
